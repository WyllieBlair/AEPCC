Australian E-Sport Porsche Cup

AEPCC Season 1

Welcome to the inaugural season of the Australian E-Sport Porsche Cup Championship (AEPCC)! Launching this October, we are bringing single-make racing to the Oceanic region, exclusively featuring the Porsche 911 GT3 Cup (992.2) on iRacing.

Our season calendar closely follows the official iRacing Porsche Cup series schedule. Prepare to race across tracks around the world and four different weekend formats ranging from 16-minute super sprints with inverted grids to longer 60-minute endurance feature races requiring compulsory pit stops.

To ensure close, competitive racing across a maximum of a 60 car grid, the field is divided into two skill-based divisions (Pro and Am) determined by a mandatory pre-qualifying week. A third class (Pro-Am) will be added if entries exceed 45. Class champions will be awarded trophies, supplemented by a scaling cash prize pool based on total season entries.

Entry is open to any driver holding an active iRacing Class D Sports Car license. To secure your spot on the grid, simply join our Discord community, complete your registration, and pay the entry fee.
