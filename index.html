Australian E-Sport Porsche Cup

AESPCC Season 1

Get ready for the inaugural season of the Australian E-Sport Porsche Cup Championship! Kicking off this October, we are bringing premier single-make racing to the Oceanic region utilizing iRacing's Porsche 911 GT3 Cup (992.2) car.

Our calendar mirrors the official iRacing Porsche Cup series, ensuring drivers can easily prepare and practice. Drivers will battle across diverse track layouts and four distinct weekend formats, ranging from 16-minute super sprints with inverted grids to 60-minute endurance features with compulsory pit stops.

With a 60-driver capacity, the field is split into three competitive driver divisions (Pro, Pro-Am, and Am) following a mandatory pre-qualifying week. Trophies await the class champions, along with an estimated $500 AUD prize pool for the Pro category.

As long as you hold a D class sports car license, you can register for the series. Pay your entry fee, join the Discord, and get your name down to secure a spot on the grid!
