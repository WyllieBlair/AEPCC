Season Announcement & Class Structure

Welcome to the official portal for the Australian Porsche Cup Championship. Competing exclusively in the Porsche 911 GT3 Cup (992.2), the championship features tiered driver divisions, dynamic race weekend configurations, live stewarding, and team championships.

Driver evaluations are finalized during pre-qualifying week to seed the field into Pro, Pro-Am, and Am classes, ensuring competitive parity right through the grid.

Weekend Formats & Sporting Regulations

Four specialized round configurations across the season calendar.

Format 1: Sprint Inverted Grid

16-minute qualifying sprint followed immediately by a 24-minute Feature race featuring an inverted top 8.

Format 2: Regular Pure Pace

Full 40-minute standalone Feature race. Grid ordered directly by qualifying times with no compulsory pit stop.

Format 3: Endurance Compulsory Stop

60-minute distance race. Fuel capacity strictly capped at 65% with mandatory pit servicing and randomized safety car windows.

Format 4: Super Sprint Triple Header

Two 20-minute heat sprints (Race 2 top-12 inverted) building directly into a 30-minute Grand Feature.

Drop Score Protocol Rule 3.4

Each driver drops their single lowest-scoring round at season end. Drop rounds do not reduce team championship scores.

License Penalties Stewarding

Drivers exceeding 6 license points incur an automated qualifying ban for the following event, resetting points down to 3.
