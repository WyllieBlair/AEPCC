Australian E-Sport Porsche Cup Championship

AEPCC Season 1

The Australian E-Sport Porsche Cup Championship (AEPCC) is an Oceanic single-make league running the Porsche 911 GT3 Cup (992.2) on iRacing. Following the official iRacing Porsche Cup calendar across eight rounds, the championship tests drivers across 40-minute regular sessions, twin-race sprints, a 60-minute Bathurst endurance round, and a three-heat finale at Road Atlanta.

Up to 60 entries are split into Pro and Am divisions via pre-season qualifying, with drivers competing for class trophies, a $350 AUD cash prize pool, and Hymo Setups subscriptions. Entry is open to all drivers holding an iRacing Class D Sports Car license or higher via the AEPCC Discord.
